Appropriate use

Education, software demonstration, model-behavior exploration, and research prototyping with non-identifying data.

Inappropriate use

Diagnosis, emergency triage, treatment selection, patient monitoring, or replacing review by a qualified healthcare professional.

Known limitations

Training-domain shift, measurement error, class imbalance, imperfect probability calibration, and locally approximate LIME explanations can all affect output.

Measurement reference

Ranges reflect the model’s accepted training-domain inputs, not clinical reference intervals.
